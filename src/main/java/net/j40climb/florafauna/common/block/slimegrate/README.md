# Slime grate farm

Craft a Mob Symbiote from a red mushroom, a brown mushroom and wheat seeds. Use it on a vanilla slime of any size. The bond lets the slime stay when you leave the pen.

Craft two grates with two rows of stick, iron bars, stick. Face down the lane to place an upright grate across it. Put a note block in the floor a few blocks from either side, leave air above both notes and give the slime a clear, fenced lane. Tap the farther note to send the slime hopping through the grate. Redstone can also play the notes.

The first crossing releases one slimeball at the foot of the grate. The slime briefly peels into two wobbling bodies and rejoins with the same identity, size and health. Put a hopper under the grate for collection. Once the slime has grown a new coat (30 seconds), play the note on the other side to harvest again. It hears notes up to ten blocks away. A covered note, jumping beside the grate, or tapping repeatedly while the coat regrows produces no slimeballs.
